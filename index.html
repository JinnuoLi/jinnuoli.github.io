About

I am a Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Security at the School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University. I received my Master of Information Security degree from the School of Computer Science, China University of Geosciences (Wuhan), in July 2026, and my Bachelor of Information Security degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in July 2023. During my master's studies, I was advised by Chi Cheng. I am now pursuing my Ph.D. under the supervision of Hao Cheng, and I also benefit from the guidance of Weijia Wang. My research interests focus on post-quantum cryptography, hardware security, and side-channel attacks.

Misc

I identified and reported the following vulnerabilities in candidates submitted to the NGCC competition:

  • Amoeba: incomplete FO ciphertext verification. The Fujisaki–Okamoto check compares only every fourth ciphertext byte, enabling chosen-ciphertext key recovery when a plaintext-checking oracle is available. Report (September 2026)
  • Lore: BCH decoding timing side channel. Secret-dependent timing in the BCH decoder enables key recovery. I reported full recovery of the Lore-L2 secret key on an STM32F4 using decapsulation timing measurements. Report (October 2026)
